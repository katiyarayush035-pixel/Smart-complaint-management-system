    Questions people ask

    I lost my tracking ID. What can I do?

    Check the "Your recent complaints" list above if you filed from this device. Otherwise, contact your college admin office and ask them to look it up by your email.

    Who can see my complaint?

    Admins see everything, including your name and email. Anyone who has your tracking ID can see the category, description and status, but never your name or email.

    What do the statuses mean?

    Pending means it is waiting for review. In Progress means someone is working on it. Resolved means the issue has been closed.

    Can I edit a complaint after submitting?

    Not yet. If you left something out, submit a new complaint and mention the earlier ID in the description.
